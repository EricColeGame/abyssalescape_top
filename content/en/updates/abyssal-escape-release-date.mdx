## Abyssal Escape Release Date Rumors vs. Confirmed Facts

Speculation spreads faster than confirmation, and release dates are the single most misreported detail in gaming. Use this table as a filter the next time you see a confident-sounding post.

| Claim you may see | Status | How to check it |
|---|---|---|
| "The release date is confirmed for [specific month]" | Almost always unverified | Look for the original post from the official account |
| "It launches this year" | Speculative unless stated by the dev | Search the official feed for the exact wording |
| "There's a Steam page with a date" | Check before believing | Search the storefront directly; do not trust screenshots |
| "The dev said it's delayed" | Possible, but needs a source | Find the Discord or social post being referenced |
| "Playtests are happening now" | Plausible and worth checking | Look for sign-up posts in the community server |
| "The game is cancelled" | Rarely true, frequently repeated | Absence of posts is not proof of cancellation |

Two habits will save you a lot of disappointment. First, always trace a claim back to a primary source — a developer post, a storefront listing, or a named outlet. Second, treat screenshots as unverified until you find the post they came from. Doctored storefront images are one of the most common forms of gaming misinformation, and they circulate for years after being debunked.
