### Habits that separate good testers from loud ones

- Change one variable at a time. Swapping both the character and the team tells you nothing.
- Record the build or version you tested on, even informally. "It felt weak" means little six weeks later.
- Test the failure case on purpose. Anyone can look strong when everything goes right.
- Ignore tier lists until you have your own data. They are useful for starting conversations, not ending them.
- Report what you find back to the community. The Abyssal Escape Fleetway conversation only improves when players contribute evidence instead of impressions.
