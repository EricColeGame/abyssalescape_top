## Practical Tips for Surviving the Abyssal Escape Deity

These tips come from **player experience** and general best practice for escape-style horror, so treat them as a starting checklist rather than gospel.

- **Map before you move.** Spend the first quiet minute learning exits, not loot.
- **Pick one route and rehearse it.** Two half-learned routes are worse than one committed one.
- **Trust audio over visuals.** The mix usually tells you where the threat is before the model does.
- **Never re-clear a finished room.** Redundancy is the most commonly reported cause of death.
- **Slow is smooth.** Deliberate movement keeps your camera stable, and a stable camera keeps you oriented.
- **Record your runs.** A capture of a failed attempt is worth more than ten forum posts.
- **Set brightness deliberately.** Too dark hides silhouettes; too bright flattens the atmosphere the design depends on.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Learn the map during calm phases | Explore during pressure phases |
| Use headphones and keep the mix audible | Play muted or with music over the game |
| Commit to a planned exit | Improvise a route mid-pursuit |
| Review footage after a failed run | Blame the game before checking your own pathing |
| Share findings with timestamps | Repeat unverified claims as fact |
