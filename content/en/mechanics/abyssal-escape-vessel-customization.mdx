## The Customization Slots That Matter Most

| Slot category | What it changes | Priority for new players | Why it matters |

### Why Accessories Deserve Extra Attention

Accessories are the most flexible part of Abyssal Escape vessel customization. ## Build Archetypes: Matching Vessel Customization to Your Playstyle

Once you understand the slots, the next step is committing to an identity. Abyssal Escape vessel customization rewards focus — a vessel that tries to do everything usually does nothing well when a chase starts. These four archetypes cover most player preferences.

### The Swap Test

Before you commit, run the same route three times with two different configurations. If your clear rate does not change, the customization is not doing meaningful work. Abyssal Escape vessel customization should produce a noticeable difference in how a run feels, not just how it looks.
